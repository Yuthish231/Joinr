What it does

  • People sign in with their directory password; Joinr never stores it.
  • Directory people are mirrored as identities, with department and job role mapped from departmentNumber and title.
  • A person disabled in the directory is deprovisioned here; a changed department or title is treated as a move.
  • New joiners get a directory entry; leavers are scrambled, removed from groups and moved to the Disabled OU; approved access adds them to the matching LDAP group.
